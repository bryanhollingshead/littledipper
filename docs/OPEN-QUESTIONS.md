| 4 | How does Big Dipper get deeper access to nodes that opt in to verification? | Options: a limited, revocable API key (`/api/v1/keys` already exists), or acting as a federated peer over `/peer/v1`. Without either, it can only reach the public and peer endpoints. | 2026-10-21 |
| 5 | Should contacts have a local "ID confirmed" mark, separate from Big Dipper verification? | Two different questions. Local: "is this node ID really the person I know?", which only the two people can settle by comparing IDs out of band. Big Dipper: "are this node's identity claims true and unchanged?" The Big Dipper check depends on the local one, since a contact asks about a node ID. Reserve the word **Verified** for Big Dipper. Decide whether a local mark is worth having, and whether a key change should clear it. | 2026-10-21 |
| 6 | Big Dipper identity verification: who delivers the result to the contact, Big Dipper or the owner's node? | The flow and principles are agreed (see Decided). Owner's lean (2026-10-23): **Big Dipper delivers the result after the owner approves sharing it.** Alternatives to weigh when Big Dipper is built: the owner's node holds the signed result and sends it peer-to-peer, with Big Dipper only answering "has this been revoked?" (Big Dipper then never learns who checks up on whom); or a middle option where Big Dipper handles consent but logs as little as possible. Other open details are in [design/IDENTITY-VERIFICATION.md](design/IDENTITY-VERIFICATION.md#still-open). | 2026-10-21 |
| 7 | How are AIs invited in, and what may they do? | Blocks AI-assisted entry and any MCP tools that write data. Covers drafts and approval, provenance, which scopes AIs get, and where AI processing runs. Owner's view: an AI is a **contact with permissions**. Leading option: show AIs in Contacts with the contact grant controls (fields, domains, current only, secret confirmation), backed by a scoped key that MCP uses, with write access only through drafts. [design/DATA-ENTRY.md](design/DATA-ENTRY.md) | 2026-10-21 |
| 8 | Messaging: the minimal first version's scope, and the organization "service" profile | Direction is agreed (see Decided). Still to settle: sign-off on the first-version scope, and how organization services are verified, identified and limited. Email beyond invites is deferred. [design/MESSAGING-AND-EMAIL.md](design/MESSAGING-AND-EMAIL.md) | 2026-10-21 |
| 9 | Receipts: date-range sharing and incentive sharing with companies | Line items are solved (the new `list` part type) and photos are now in backups. Grants still need date ranges. Company incentive sharing probably follows the AI-contacts model (7). [design/RECEIPTS.md](design/RECEIPTS.md) | 2026-10-21 |
| 10 | Financial transactions: when to build, data model, and how data gets in | Wanted at some point. Accounts stay a hand-kept list; transactions become their own collection with date-range grants (shared answer with 9). Suggested intake: CSV/OFX/QFX file import first, then SimpleFIN Bridge (US/Canada) and GoCardless (UK/EU) as owner-held connectors; watch the US Section 1033 open-banking rule; Plaid and similar are a poor fit because they license companies, not individuals, and routing through Big Dipper would expose bank data. [design/FINANCIAL-ACCOUNTS.md](design/FINANCIAL-ACCOUNTS.md) | 2026-10-22 |
| 11 | Invoices and payments: companies send invoices, owners pay from an embedded stablecoin wallet or bank account | Long term; seeds only. To decide: self-custody or a provider, and the regulation that follows (money transmission, KYC); which rails (USDC on which chains, ACH or open banking pay-by-bank); how the payee is verified (Big Dipper organization verification); how payment is confirmed and turned into a receipt; and how invoices travel (a message `kind` over the organization service). [design/RECEIPTS.md](design/RECEIPTS.md#later-invoices-and-payments-seeds-only) | 2026-10-22 |
| 12 | Data commons: owners voluntarily contribute anonymized data (receipts first), possibly for a reward | Long-term vision. To decide: anonymization method (leading idea: itemized contributions of SKU, unit price and payment-method type, with coarse date and region, broken out of baskets and batched; federated analytics rejected), who governs the commons (a cooperative or trust separate from Big Dipper), how contributors are paid and fake data prevented, honest consent about what can't be withdrawn, and legal review. [design/DATA-COMMONS.md](design/DATA-COMMONS.md) | 2026-10-22 |
| 13 | Health data: restructure into coded domains so it can be compared and shared | Today's `health` domain is a flat free-text list. Direction: follow FHIR (conditions, medications, allergies, immunizations, procedures, labs, visits, care plans and outcomes) with standard codes (SNOMED CT, ICD-10, RxNorm, LOINC, CVX), import through SMART on FHIR patient access. Needs links between records (a treatment for a condition), which the domain engine lacks. Health commons: per-study consent, legal review. [design/HEALTH.md](design/HEALTH.md) | 2026-10-22 |
| 14 | How do skins (third-party UX kits and apps) get access? | The bundled UI signs in with a session cookie and passkeys tied to the node's own address. A skin hosted elsewhere needs: an authorization flow (the skin asks for scopes, the owner approves on their node, the skin gets a key, as in OAuth with PKCE), CORS for approved origins, and a decision on which session-only actions a skin may ever do (full-owner skins vs. limited apps). Skins served by the node itself (`LD_WEB_DIST`) already work. [COMPATIBILITY.md](COMPATIBILITY.md) XI | 2026-10-22 |
| 15 | Big Dipper managed upgrades: how updates actually reach each kind of host | The owner experience is built (Settings → Big Dipper): the owner says how the node is hosted (cloud platform, Big Dipper one-click install, Docker, set up by hand, device at home, not sure), and that decides which modes are offered (tell me; security fixes on their own; everything on its own) and when they may run. To decide when Big Dipper is built: the mechanism per host (deploy hooks on Render, Railway and Fly; a small updater beside Docker and home installs; instructions for hand-built servers), how Big Dipper tests a release before it is offered, backup and rollback before each update, how security fixes are told apart from other releases, and whether detection can replace asking. Compare with the existing `LD_UPDATE_FEED_URL` and `LD_DEPLOY_HOOK_URL`. | 2026-10-23 |
| 16 | Big Dipper ID verification: provider, method and pricing | Decided so far (2026-10-23): the Little Dipper owner pays once to verify, plus periodic re-checks (yearly by default; every two years or on request are offered). Big Dipper keeps no ID documents, only the result and salted hashes of the checked values, compared against the values on the owner's node. Still open: which verification provider or method (document plus liveness check, government digital IDs, bank-based identity), the prices, what a re-check involves, assurance levels, and the standards question in [design/IDENTITY-VERIFICATION.md](design/IDENTITY-VERIFICATION.md#still-open). | 2026-10-23 |

## Decided

| Decision | Date | Details |
|---|---|---|
| Big Dipper sends every request to share a verification result to the owner for permission, in their own Little Dipper app. Big Dipper never stores ID documents, only the result and salted hashes of the verified values. | 2026-10-21 | [design/IDENTITY-VERIFICATION.md](design/IDENTITY-VERIFICATION.md) |
| Human data entry uses tables, not per-record forms. Little Dipper will be MCP compatible. A documents module (admin and API) is a key upcoming feature. AI-assisted entry waits for the AI invitation design. | 2026-10-21 | [design/DATA-ENTRY.md](design/DATA-ENTRY.md) |
| Receipts will be a built-in domain with file attachments (first version built 2026-10-22, ahead of documents). It's for taxes, buying again, warranties, proof of purchase and opt-in incentive sharing. It starts with raw data only; links to other categories come later. Capture: table entry and upload first, then email forwarding, with merchants sending receipts directly much later. | 2026-10-21 | [design/RECEIPTS.md](design/RECEIPTS.md) |
| One inbox, organized by contact. Node-to-node messaging starts minimal, but its data model supports rich messaging later. Organizations won't run nodes: they're verified, added as contacts, and implement a messaging-only API service. Invites stay as they are; other email options come later. | 2026-10-21 | [design/MESSAGING-AND-EMAIL.md](design/MESSAGING-AND-EMAIL.md) |
| Documents: a distinct page plus documents beside their entries in My data; assistants read documents and propose entries the owner accepts; expiring share links for people without a Little Dipper; no drop links for now. First version built. | 2026-10-22 | [design/DOCUMENTS.md](design/DOCUMENTS.md) |
